Chicken Noodle

Chicken noodle is a popular comfort soup, typically featuring chicken, noodles, and vegetables (like carrots, celery, and onions) simmered in a seasoned broth.

Ingredients :

  • Noodles (instant or egg noodle)
  • Chicken (sliced)
  • Garlic (schoped)
  • Vegetables (carrot, cabbage, or green onion)
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Soy Sauce
  • Oil

Steps :

  1. Cook noodles
  2. Cook chicken
  3. Add vegetables
  4. Mix noodles
  5. Stir well
