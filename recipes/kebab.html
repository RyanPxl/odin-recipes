Kebab

A kebab is a diverse dish of seasoned meat—traditionally lamb, but also beef, chicken, or fish—that is grilled, roasted, or stewed, often on skewers over an open flame.

Ingredients :

  • Tortilla wraps
  • Sliced beef
  • Lettuce (choped)
  • Tomato (sliced)
  • Cucumber (optional)
  • Mayonnaise
  • Sauce

Steps :

  1. Coot the meat
  2. Prepare veggies
  3. Warm the tortilla
  4. Assemble
  5. Wrap it
